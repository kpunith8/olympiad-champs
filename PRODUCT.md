# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Planned: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Kinde, Neon PostgreSQL and Vercel.

## Users and purpose

Children in India in Classes 1–10 learn Maths, Science and English for Olympiad preparation, primarily independently with optional parent help. They should understand a concept, practise, take a sample test and resume later.

## Constraints

Questions will be supplied later; 35-question scope and scoring are undecided. Learning is intended to be free; premium practice and tests are under consideration. Account ownership and parental consent are undecided. Do not invent exam affiliation, content or outcomes.

## Brand and accessibility

Olympiad Champs has no approved visual identity or assets. Design for accessible touch, keyboard and screen-reader use across ages and devices. The owner chose image-led concepts for future landing pages; app screens are code-led.
